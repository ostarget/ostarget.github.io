Powered by Genesis™

At the heart of OSTarget is the Genesis™ Integrity Engine, a proprietary cryptographic verification layer designed to protect the integrity of investigative and operational records from creation through final reporting.

Genesis™ cryptographically links case notes, evidence files, timeline events, audit entries, and report generation activity to create a verifiable record of what was entered, when it was entered, and whether protected records have been altered, removed, or tampered with after the fact.

Rather than relying solely on conventional audit logs or user permissions, the Genesis™ Integrity Engine supports evidentiary continuity, chain of custody, supervisory review, disclosure, and court-ready reporting by helping users verify that operational records remain complete, authentic, and accountable throughout their lifecycle.

The Genesis™ Integrity Engine forms the trust layer of the OSTarget platform, giving investigators, organizations, and legal professionals greater confidence that every protected operational record can be independently verified for integrity whenever required.
